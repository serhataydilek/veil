# Product principles

## Silence by default

Veil reveals only local facts and protocol facts that are safe to disclose. Entering, saving, deleting, or retrying a contact ID never says whether another identity exists, is reachable, has acted, or has blocked someone. Use neutral local language such as **ID saved** and **Unable to use this ID**; never present a request, approval, waiting, lookup, presence, read, or delivery-to-person state.

## No social mechanics

Veil has no friends, followers, profiles, public directory, discovery, address-book import, suggestions, recent people, nearby people, groups, or social graph. A relationship begins only after both people independently exchange and enter current IDs.

## Device-owned identity

An identity is created and held on this device. Veil has no login, password, account recovery, cloud restore, or migration. Reinstalling, clearing data, loss of protected key material, or device loss can permanently remove the identity. Product copy says **Create identity**, never sign in or restore account.

## Ephemerality is normal

Text messages are available for at most 24 hours from creation. This is mandatory product behavior, not a selectable disappearing-message feature. Ambiguous time fails closed and may remove messages early.

## Local names, private relationships

Aliases are optional device-local labels. They are neither usernames nor peer-visible data. Public IDs are temporary pairing capabilities, not lasting conversation names or identity proof.

## Honest privacy claims

Veil protects plaintext from the relay but does not promise anonymity, untraceability, forensic-impossible deletion, or protection from an unlocked/compromised device or a peer who retains received text. Notifications are wake hints, not message state.
