# Accessibility

- Support scalable system text without clipping, inaccessible composer growth, or lost destructive controls.
- Provide screen-reader labels and logical reading/focus order for all actions, local status, IDs, QR alternatives, and dialogs.
- Meet sufficient contrast in both themes; never communicate trust, warning, destructive action, or selection by color alone.
- Meet platform touch-target guidance; preserve clear focus and pressed states.
- Manual entry is always an alternative to QR. IDs use grouped chunks readable aloud; future ID encoding should avoid visually similar characters where possible.
- Respect motion-reduction settings and avoid motion required to understand a state.
- Destructive confirmations identify consequence and action clearly, use default cancel, and do not rely on gesture-only confirmation.

Accessibility services can access content according to platform/user configuration; Veil must not imply that app visuals can override those platform permissions.
